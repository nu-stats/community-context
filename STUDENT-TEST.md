# Testing the Immigration and Crime: Community Context site (about 30 minutes)

Thank you for testing this. We want to know where a student gets stuck. There are no right or wrong answers and nothing is graded. Do not ask anyone for help while you work; write down what you did instead. The site also has a "Test this site" tab with the questions below and a button that saves your answers as a text file.

You need: the site open in a browser and the assignment (`community-context.md`).

## What to do

Use the site the way you would for the assignment, with the real Census data.

1. Pick a group (country of birth) that you know something about, for example where your family or a friend's family comes from.
2. On **Start here**, choose the group, a ranking period, and three indicators from the menu. (Population and foreign_born_percent are given.)
3. Read the **Top 50** tab, then the **Data** tab, then the **Weighted averages** tab.
4. Try a different ranking period, or a different group, and see what changes.
5. Download the Top 50 table as CSV and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

Write down anything that confused you as you go.

## Please answer

1. What was the first thing you did not understand?
2. Was there a word or label you had to guess at? Which one?
3. After choosing your group, did you know what to do next, or did you look for instructions?
4. Which step took the longest, and why?
5. Did anything look wrong, or not match the assignment text?
6. What one change would make this easier to use?

Also write down your device and browser, and the group you tested.
